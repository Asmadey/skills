# Voice Calibration & Matching

AI humanization is not just about removing bad patterns; it's about matching the real human voice of the author.

## Analysis Checklist

If the user provides a writing sample, analyze it before rewriting:

1. **Sentence Rhythm**
   - Are sentences short and punchy?
   - Long and flowing?
   - A mix of both?
2. **Word Choice**
   - Casual and conversational?
   - Academic and precise?
   - Technical and jargon-heavy?
3. **Paragraph Structure**
   - Do they jump right into the point?
   - Do they set context first?
4. **Punctuation Habits**
   - Frequent use of dashes?
   - Many parenthetical asides?
   - Semicolons for complex ideas?
5. **Transitions**
   - Explicit connectors ("Furthermore," "However")?
   - Natural, implied transitions?

## Execution Rules

- **Don't just remove AI patterns** - replace them with patterns from the sample.
- If they write short sentences, don't produce long ones.
- If they use casual language like "stuff" or "things," don't upgrade to "elements" or "components."
- If no sample is provided, fallback to the **Personality and Soul** guidelines in the main `SKILL.md`.
