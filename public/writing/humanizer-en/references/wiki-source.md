# Wikipedia: Signs of AI Writing

This skill is based on the [Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) guide, maintained by **WikiProject AI Cleanup**.

## Source Context
The patterns documented in this skill come from collective observations of thousands of AI-generated edits on Wikipedia. These patterns are not just "stylistic choices" but are statistically high-probability sequences that LLMs tend to generate when attempting to be "helpful" or "encyclopedic."

## Key Insight
> "LLMs use statistical algorithms to guess what should come next. The result tends toward the most statistically likely result that applies to the widest variety of cases."

By identifying and removing these "statistically likely" patterns, we can restore the unique, unpredictable, and specific nature of human writing.
